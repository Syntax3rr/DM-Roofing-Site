---
title: Soffit & Fascia
icon: layers
excerpt: Soffit covers the underside of your eaves and fascia runs along the edge. We replace both, usually along with the eavestroughs.
order: 3
---

Soffit keeps birds and squirrels out of your eaves and lets your attic breathe. Fascia protects the ends of the rafters and holds up the eavestroughs. When one is in rough shape, the other usually is too, so we often do them together.
